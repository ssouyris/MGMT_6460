Solvers

Commercial

  • Gurobi
  • IBM CPLEX
  • FICO Xpress, MOSEK, COPT, Knitro, BARON, Hexaly

Open source

  • HiGHS: LP, MIP
  • SCIP: MIP, MINLP
  • CBC, GLPK, OR-Tools (Google), Ipopt, cuOpt

Not an exhaustive list.

A modeling language states the model; a solver finds the optimum. Pyomo, JuMP and PuLP hand the same model to any of the solvers on the right. gurobipy talks to Gurobi only. Solver comparisons: Mittelmann’s benchmarks, https://plato.asu.edu/bench.html.

GurobiPy and the Gurobi Optimizer

Modeling API: gurobipy

A Python package: variables, constraints and objective are Python expressions over index sets.

Solver: Gurobi Optimizer

Solves LP and MIP models: branch and bound plus presolve, cuts and heuristics.

Notebook: Potato Farms in GurobiPy

Open fixed_charge_farms.ipynb in Google Colab

Lecture 02’s fixed charge model, in the sections of Gurobi’s modeling examples. Run the cells from top to bottom.

Formulation gurobipy
\(y_i \in \{0, 1\}\) y = m.addVars(farms, vtype=GRB.BINARY)
\(x_{ij} \ge 0\) x = m.addVars(routes)
\(\min \sum_i f_i y_i + \sum_{i,j} c_{ij} x_{ij}\) m.setObjective(y.prod(fixed_cost) + x.prod(ship_cost))
\(\sum_j x_{ij} \le s_i y_i \;\; \forall i\) m.addConstrs(x.sum(i, "*") <= harvest[i]*y[i] for i in farms)
\(\sum_i x_{ij} = d_j \;\; \forall j\) m.addConstrs(x.sum("*", j) == capacity[j] for j in plants)

Result: farms 1, 2, 4 and 6 for $2,082,300, the Excel answer.
